How it works

Three steps, no group-chat math required

01

Start a cart, share the link

Anyone can start a group cart and send an invite link over WhatsApp or SMS — no downloads needed for the people joining.

02

Everyone adds their own items

Each person adds what they want. The cart updates live for the whole group, with each item tagged to who added it.

03

Pay your share, get one delivery

The bill splits itself by what each person added. Everyone pays their own share, and the group gets a single delivery.
